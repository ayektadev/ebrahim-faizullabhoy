About

Hi, I'm Ebrahim! I'm a born-and-bred Georgia boy from Atlanta, now living in the New York metro area for medical school. My interest in medicine began when I was thirteen, during a trip to Yemen with my parents, who had gone with an NGO to help build wells and distribute food. While we were there, I happened to see a U.S. medical team caring for people with very scarce medical resources. It was one of my first experiences seeing how access to basic resources could shape a community's health. As I grew older, I began recognizing many of the same challenges closer to home. That connection between health needs abroad and those in my own community has stayed with me and is a large part of what draws me to global surgery today.

I studied biology at Georgia Tech before moving to New Jersey for medical school at Rutgers New Jersey Medical School. My interests center on global surgery, trauma, and delivering care in resource-limited settings. I'm particularly interested in the systems that make surgical care possible, from staffing and supplies to medical records and long-term follow-up. Much of my research explores how we can strengthen these systems in ways that are sustainable and locally driven. I've also enjoyed putting these interests into practice through projects involving surgical information systems, wilderness medicine, and community first-aid education.

What appeals to me most about global surgery is that the skills and approaches are transferable. The challenges facing a rural hospital abroad may look different from those facing an underserved community here, but many of the underlying barriers are similar. I hope to build a career that allows me to work across both settings.

Outside of medicine, I enjoy skiing, camping, cooking, and spending time outdoors whenever I get the chance.

Projects

Flagship work

CESIS

Founder · 2026 – present

The Center for Equitable Surgical Information Systems is building toward surgical data infrastructure that partner hospitals own and govern themselves. Its first project recovered twelve years of handwritten operative records from Kabala, Sierra Leone, and is now being validated and extended to partner sites in Ghana and Peru.

Ayekta

Founder · 2025 – present

A social entrepreneurship effort building a modular, offline-first electronic medical record shaped around what surgical teams actually need, from preoperative evaluation through follow-up.

Cramium Education

Founder · pre-seed startup · 2026 – present

An AI-native study platform for medical students that turns course materials into study guides, flashcards, and board-style questions.

NJ WildMed

Founder · 2025 – present

Wilderness and preventive medicine teaching in Essex County: heat and cold illness, disaster preparedness, and first aid. Funded by a Pozen grant.

NJMS Trauma Surgery Interest Group

Founder · 2026 – present

Hands-on suturing, airway, and resuscitation sessions, plus mentorship and shadowing with trauma faculty and residents.
